A month calendar for the wrist. Today sits in an accent pill, and each day of the coming week with events gets a dot
in its calendar's colour (up to three per day). Turn the crown or tap **<** and **>** to change the month; tap the
month's name to come back to today.

It can also live next to the watch face: long-press **Calendar** in Apps and choose **Add page** for a full-screen view
of today and this week, or **Add widget** for a card with the date and today's event count.

The events come from your phone: the watch asks when you open the app and every 5 minutes while it runs, and keeps the
last answer. Only times and calendar colours reach the watch, never titles. The phone sends the next 7 days, so days
further on have no dots.

The first time, the watch says "allow on your phone": allow Calendar in FMC Companion's Permissions screen.

## Requirements

- The [patched firmware](/firmware) with the channel key (2026-11-13 or later) and the [App Loader](/firmware)
  (2026-11-15 or later for the widget and the page).
- **FMC Companion** on the phone the watch is paired with (iOS or Android), with calendar access allowed.
