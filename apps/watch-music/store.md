# Watch Music

Your phone's music, podcasts and whatever is playing right now, on your wrist. Pick a song, a podcast episode or the
app that is already playing, and control it from a clean full-screen player that glows in the colours of the album
art.

The start screen shows only what your phone actually has: **Control media**, **Music**, **Podcasts** and **Custom**.
When only one of them has content, the app opens straight into it, so there is nothing to tap through.

## What it does

- **Control media**: control whatever is playing on the phone, from music apps to video apps and podcast players.
  Artist, title, play / pause, previous, next and a progress ring, with the glow taken from the artwork when the
  app provides it.
- **Music**: a smooth song list you scroll with the crown or a drag. Tap a song and it plays on the phone.
- **Podcasts**: every podcast you add in Watch Menu 2 is a row. Tap one to open its episode player, and use previous
  and next to move between episodes.
- **Custom**: run the shortcuts you saved on the phone straight from the watch.

## Player

- **Animated album-colour background.** The cover's colours glow behind the player and the light slowly drifts, so
  the screen feels alive without draining the watch.
- **Heart to save a song.** Tap the heart to save what is playing, from any app. It turns red whenever a saved song
  comes on, and a second tap unsaves it.
- **Double-tap to skip 5 seconds.** Double-tap the left side of the player to jump back 5 seconds, or the right side to
  jump forward 5 seconds. Tap away from the buttons, so they keep working as usual.
- **Volume on the crown.** Tap the speaker button, then turn the crown. The button lights up and the title shows the
  level; it switches off by itself about a second after you stop turning.
- **Progress ring.** One smooth ring around play / pause shows how far into the song you are.
- **Long-press to go back.** Press and hold anywhere on the player to return to the list.
- **Add it as a page.** Long-press Watch Music in Apps and choose Add page to keep the player a swipe away from the
  watch face.

## Requirements

- The **Watch Menu 2** app on your Android phone, version 1.0.126 or newer. Watch Music is its watch app: the phone
  app provides your songs, podcasts, custom actions and the active media session, and carries every command back to
  your phone. [Download Watch Menu 2](https://github.com/abdalrahmanmamdouh2004-svg/watch-menu/releases/latest).
  Open it once and grant the notification access it asks for, so it can see what is playing.
- The watch connected to your phone through Watch Menu 2, over the same Bluetooth link as Nothing X.
- The [patched firmware](/firmware) with the channel key and the [App Loader](/firmware)
  (ABI 1.3 or newer).
- Android 7.0 or newer.
